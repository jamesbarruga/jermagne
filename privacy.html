Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who uses it

Jermagne is used by one person, my wife, on her own Mac. Nobody else signs in to it.

What it reads

With her permission, it reads her Google Drive files, Gmail, Calendar, Chat, Contacts, Docs and Sheets so it can answer her questions and prepare drafts for her.

Where the data goes

Her Google data stays on her Mac. It isn't sold, shared or kept on any server of mine. To write answers, parts of what she asks about are sent to the AI service the app runs on, under that service's own terms.

What it won't do on its own

It never sends an email or message, changes her calendar, or deletes or shares a file without asking her first.

Removing access

She can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
